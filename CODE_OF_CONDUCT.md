# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Contributions from people with different experience levels and backgrounds are welcome.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people. Respect privacy and do not share another person's private information.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on [@josephadogeriopao's profile](https://github.com/josephadogeriopao). Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportional and protect the privacy of those involved.
